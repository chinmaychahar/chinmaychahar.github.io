I work at Amazon in the Alexa AI team, where I build scalable backend systems in Java and Python, focusing on LLM inference optimization and automated prompt engineering pipelines. My day-to-day involves working on low-latency ML systems and multilingual NLU at scale. Previously, I was a Cloud Consultant Intern at AWS, building serverless APIs with Lambda and automating cloud infrastructure.

Before Amazon, I worked as a Machine Learning Intern at IIT Bombay under [Dr. Venkatapathy Subramanian](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=GAMMJW8AAAAJ&hl=en), building Transformer-based OCR pipelines for 6+ Indian languages and contributing to the digital preservation of historical Sanskrit texts. The work involved processing 80,000+ documents and deploying deep learning models for table detection in historical manuscripts.

In open source, I help maintain the [`opentelemetry-aws`](https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-rust-contrib/tree/main/opentelemetry-aws) Rust crate under the CNCF OpenTelemetry project, and contribute to other CNCF and AcademySoftwareFoundation projects like Meshery, MaterialX, and Dragonfly.

Outside of work, I'm an avid fiction reader and currently writing my first book, exploring storytelling from a new perspective.
