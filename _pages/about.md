I work at Amazon in the Alexa AI team, where I help build scalable backend systems in Java and Python, particularly LLM inference optimization and automated prompt engineering pipelines. My team works on low-latency ML systems and multilingual NLU at scale. Previously, I was a Cloud Consultant Intern at AWS, building serverless APIs with Lambda and automating cloud infrastructure.

Before Amazon, I worked as a Machine Learning Intern at IIT Bombay under [Dr. Venkatapathy Subramanian](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=GAMMJW8AAAAJ&hl=en), building Transformer-based OCR pipelines for 6+ Indian languages and contributing to the digital preservation of historical Sanskrit texts. The work involved processing 80K+ documents and deploying deep learning models for table detection in historical manuscripts.

I also help maintain the [`opentelemetry-aws`](https://github.com/open-telemetry/opentelemetry-rust-contrib/tree/main/opentelemetry-aws) Rust crate under the CNCF OpenTelemetry project, and contribute to other CNCF and AcademySoftwareFoundation projects. Outside of work, I avidly read detective thrillers, astronomy, and history. Someday, I plan to write a book that explores storytelling from a new perspective.
