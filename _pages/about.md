Cloud Consultant at Amazon Web Services (AWS), where I specialize in cloud infrastructure and platform engineering. 
My experience spans infrastructure as code, container orchestration, and cloud-native technologies.

My research work at IIT Bombay, under the guidance of [Dr. Venkatapathy Subramanian](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=GAMMJW8AAAAJ&hl=en), focused on developing 
state-of-the-art OCR models and corresponding corpus pipelines for multiple Indian languages. I developed 
word-level Machine Learning pipelines based on Transformer-based OCR models for six Indian languages. The work 
involved extensive data analysis, collation and pre-processing of Indian preserved texts, with particular focus 
on Sanskrit documents. Successfully deployed open-source deep learning models for table detection and recognition 
in document images and PDFs, contributing to the digital preservation of historical texts.

My focus remains on designing and implementing efficient cloud solutions, building systems that effectively 
bridge research innovations with practical applications.
