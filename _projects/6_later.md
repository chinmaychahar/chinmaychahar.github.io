A tiny, self-hosted take on FutureMe. Write a letter, pick a delivery date, and seal it. The text is encrypted on disk and emailed back to you when the day arrives.

### Features

- Sealed, encrypted letters (peeking early carries a cooling-off period)
- No accounts or cloud, everything stays on your machine
- A daily job delivers each letter by email on its chosen date

### Tech Stack

- Python (Flask)
- SMTP email delivery
